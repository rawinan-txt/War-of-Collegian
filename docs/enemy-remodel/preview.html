War of Collegian - Enemy preview (static, review build)

All nine enemies next to Bug Hunter. The five "in game" enemies are the current roster; the four "concept only" teaching-staff characters are not in gameplay. Sprites are 64x64 chibi pixel art with a dark inner and a white outer outline. No animation yet.

Side by side with Bug Hunter (2x, as drawn in battle)

Enemies are mirrored in the game so they face the heroes. The Final Exam boss is drawn 3.3x in the battle (2 x the 1.65 boss scale); Exam Master has no in-game scale yet.
