War of Collegian - Enemy preview (animated build)

All nine enemies next to Bug Hunter, with the real animation sheets playing at their real frame rates. Stage 1 has Assignment, Deadline, Group, Midterm and the Final Exam boss; Stage 2 (Faculty Staff) adds Strict Professor, Ruthless TA, Thesis Advisor and the Exam Master boss. Frame 0 of every idle sheet is the approved static design.

Side by side with Bug Hunter (2x, as drawn in battle)

Enemies are mirrored in the game so they face the heroes. The Final Exam is drawn 3.3x and the Exam Master 3.0x in battle (visual only; hitbox, range and targeting do not use the sprite size).
